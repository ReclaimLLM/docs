## Investigate and export events

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Pick the period you want to review, for example 15 Sep – 16 Oct 2026.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Filter by category, outcome, actor ID, or target type. Choose the **Policy** category to isolate policy changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an event">
    Click any event to open its detail drawer: outcome, timestamp, identity, target, metadata, and request context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to CSV">
    Click **Export CSV** to download the filtered events for your security review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long are audit events kept?">
    365 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is viewing sensitive data logged?">
    Yes. For example, viewing model analysis results is recorded as its own sensitive-access event, as is encrypted session access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export the audit log?">
    Yes. Apply filters and click Export CSV.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
