ReclaimLLM is built on a simple premise: sessions you capture belong to you. That means private by default, no training on your data, and real controls to export, delete, encrypt, and choose where your history lives — not just a policy statement, but enforced defaults and features you can act on today.

## Privacy defaults

Every account starts with the most protective settings. Nothing changes without you choosing it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Private by default" icon="lock">
    Every captured session is private to your account. Nothing is shared, sold, or visible to anyone else unless you explicitly share it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never used for training" icon="shield-halved">
    RCLM does not use your session data to train models — not now, not in the future, not without explicit consent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Export on your terms" icon="file-export">
    Export your full history in standard formats (JSONL, HuggingFace, and more) at any time. No lock-in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete on demand" icon="trash">
    Delete individual sessions or your entire account and all associated data whenever you want.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Control summary

The table below shows the default state of every privacy-relevant setting. None of these require any action — they are active from the moment you create an account.

| Control | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Session visibility | Private | Visible only to you unless shared |
| Data used for training | Never | No exceptions, no opt-in |
| Sensitive content detection | Enabled (Paid) | Flags credentials, tokens, PII |
| Export | Available | JSONL, HuggingFace, and more |
| Deletion | Available | Per-session or full account |
| Encrypted raw session storage | Off (opt-in) | Paid and Enterprise |
| Data residency | Default region | US or EU on Paid; on-prem on Enterprise |
| Recovery key storage | Not stored | Downloaded once; never emailed |

## Privacy controls by plan

<Accordion title="Free plan">
  - Sessions private by default
  - Export full history (JSON)
  - Delete any session or your account
  - Global redaction substitutions for hook capture
  - Manual sensitive content review before sharing
  - Default region storage
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Paid plan">
  Everything in Free, plus:
  - Sensitive data detection and auto-redaction via hook rules
  - Encrypted raw session storage with a one-time recovery key download
  - Data residency: choose US or EU
  - Full usage history and trends
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Enterprise plan">
  Everything in Paid, plus:
  - Org-wide encrypted session storage with admin-controlled decrypt access
  - DLP scanning and policy enforcement (org-wide rules coming soon)
  - Configurable data retention and legal hold
  - On-premises deployment option
  - Role-based access: admin, team lead, developer
</Accordion>
