Rolling out AI coding tools means organizing who uses what. The Teams page is the membership page: admins create teams and add members by email, and team leads send a developer invite from the same page. The old Members URL opens Teams.

## Set up a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the team">
    At the top of the Teams page, type a name in the inline field and click **Create Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a member">
    On the team's card, click **Add member**, enter the teammate's email, and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust their role">
    The add form has no role selector. Change the role afterwards from the **Role** dropdown on the member's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open team Signals">
    Click **View Signals** to see issues concentrated in that team, or a clean empty state when there's nothing to act on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I set a role when adding a member?">
    Not in the add form. Add the member by email, then change their role from the Role dropdown on their row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does an empty team Signals view mean?">
    Nothing is concentrated in that team, so no action is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove someone from a team?">
    Yes. Use Remove from team on the member's row.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
