The Members tab (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/members`) is where admins and team leads manage who belongs to the org.

## What you'll see

- An **Add new member** form — email \+ role (admin only; team leads can only invite developers).
- A built-in **role guide** explaining what Developer, Team Lead, and Admin can each do.
- A **members table** — Name, Email, Role, Joined, Last session — with an inline role selector and a Remove action per row (you can't change your own role or remove yourself).
- A **pending invites** table (when any exist) — Email, Role, Expires — with Copy link, Resend, and Cancel actions.

## What you can do

Send an invite, change a member's role, remove a member from the org, and copy/resend/cancel a pending invite.

## Who sees it

Visible to **admin** and **team\_lead** roles. Team leads can only invite new members as Developers; only admins can invite as Team Lead or Admin.

For the full role permission matrix, see [Roles & Access](/enterprise/roles-access).
