The ReclaimLLM enterprise tier wraps a governance layer around the AI work your developers are already doing. Without changing how individuals capture sessions, your organization gains cross-developer analytics, a governed LLM gateway, role-based access control, org-wide encrypted session storage, and the audit trail you need for compliance and incident response.

If your developers use coding assistants and LLM APIs today, you already have four problems whether you know it or not.

  <Card title="You don't know what it's costing you" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">

  </Card>

  <Card title="You have no audit trail" icon="file-magnifying-glass">
    For regulated industries or incident response, answering "what did our developers share with external AI providers over the past 90 days?" requires a capture layer. Without one, the question is simply unanswerable.

</CardGroup>

## How the org layer works

When a developer joins your organization, their sessions are tagged server-side with durable org and team attribution. You don't need to change their local capture tools.

- **Historical backfill** — existing sessions can be attributed to your org when a developer joins, so the enterprise view covers past and future activity from day one.
- **Team assignment** — admins assign developers to teams, and attribution flows automatically from that point forward.
- **Non-disruptive rollout** — enabling the enterprise layer does not change individual developer capture tools. Everything each developer already has set up continues to work as before.
