The User Portal is your signed-in workspace for everything ReclaimLLM captures under your account. Use it to find past sessions, inspect what an agent did, organize reusable work, and manage account-level controls.

## Open the portal

Sign in at [reclaimllm.com/login](https://reclaimllm.com/login). The top navigation gives you direct access to **Dashboard**, **Stats**, **Datasets**, **Settings**, and **Feedback**.

<Note>
  The User Portal contains your personal account experience. Organization admins and team leads use the separate [Enterprise Portal](/enterprise/overview) for organization-wide sessions, analytics, membership, governance, and gateway controls.
</Note>

## Availability

Your plan controls some actions inside the portal. Free accounts can capture, browse, and search sessions. Paid and Enterprise plans add capabilities such as full-history analytics, semantic features, and encrypted storage. See [Plans](/plans/overview) for the current comparison.
