Compliance in ReclaimLLM is an **evidence report** for AI coding controls — not a SOC 2 attestation or certification. It turns coverage, anomalies, gateway usage, offboarding, and alert delivery into daily control statuses that auditors can rely on without seeing prompts, code, or secrets.

<Warning>
  ReclaimLLM does **not** certify your organization. Snapshots and exports are designed for auditor reliance; your assessor still owns the attestation opinion.
</Warning>

## What you get

| Capability | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Control catalog (RC-AI-*)** | Mapped to TSC (and ISO / NIST AI RMF tags) with explicit pass / warn / fail / na rules |
| **Daily snapshots** | Nightly evaluation at 02:00 org time; admins can **Compute preview now** |
| **Population drill-down** | Content-free IDs that made up the latest evaluation |
| **CSV evidence export** | Period-bounded pack of control results for auditors |
| **Access reviews** | Quarterly (or off-cycle) campaigns over keys and members with attested completion |
| **Risk exceptions** | Time-boxed accepted risk with required expiry (≤12 months) |
| **Offboarding hooks** | Member removal revokes owned keys and opens shared-key exposure reviews |

## Guardrails

1. **Content-free evidence** — IDs, counts, categories, timestamps, and file *patterns* only. Never prompts, code, commands, or secret values.
2. **Detection is automatic; enforcement is human** — revoke / rotate / dismiss decisions stay on an admin or reviewer.
3. **Previews before irreversible actions** where the product already requires confirmation (for example key revoke).
4. **Legal hold** — compliance retention never deletes under legal hold; lowering compliance retention warns on Control health (RC-AI-10).

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Met the control threshold for that day |
| **warn** | Near miss, soft miss, or recorded SoD / retention exception |
| **fail** | Missed the control criteria |
| **na** | Not applicable (no population, feature not enabled, or enterprise prerequisite missing) |
| **pending** | No snapshot yet for that control |

## How to check a control

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Control health">
    Go to **Compliance → Control health** (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/compliance`). Admins and team leads can read it. Developers do not see this tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh today's result">
    Nightly snapshots run at **02:00** org time. An admin can click **Compute preview now** to evaluate every control for today immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open View population">
    Click **View population** on that row. The panel lists the evidence for the evaluation: counts, categories, and IDs. It never includes prompts, file contents, or secret values. Team leads see team-filtered rows for RC-AI-01, RC-AI-03, and RC-AI-04. Every other control is org-wide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **View population** evaluates today. The status badge is the latest stored snapshot, which can be yesterday until the nightly job or **Compute preview now** runs.
</Note>

## What each control means

### RC-AI-01 — DLP enabled on enrolled devices

Share of expected people whose device has DLP on. A person is expected when they are a developer or team lead and they have a recent session, a device check-in, or a non-retired device.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | At least 98% covered |
| **warn** | At least 90% and below 98% |
| **fail** | Below 90% |
| **na** | No expected people |

**View population** shows one row, **Org DLP coverage on** that day. On fail or warn, the panel states how many expected people were covered and links to [Coverage](/enterprise/coverage). A usual miss is someone with recent activity and no enrolled device (`no_device`), a stale device, or DLP turned off.

**Check it:** enroll a device for each active developer and team lead and leave DLP on, then **Compute preview now**. Open Coverage to see the uncovered person. Removing the last expected device makes the control **na**.

### RC-AI-02 — Sensitive reads redacted before transmission

Sensitive file reads should be redacted or blocked before they leave the device. The control uses sensitive file-access events when they exist, and AD-004 findings otherwise.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | DLP gap ratio ≤ 2% and every gap finding is triaged |
| **warn** | Gap ratio ≤ 4%, or at least 90% of gap findings are triaged |
| **fail** | Gap ratio above 4% and triage below 90% |
| **na** | No sensitive reads in the window |

**View population** lists the sensitive reads or AD-004 findings, with the session link, matched pattern, and DLP gap counts.

**Check it:** read a sensitive file (for example a `.env`) with DLP on, then preview. The row should show the read as covered. Turn DLP off for one sensitive read and leave the finding open to see **warn** or **fail**. Disposition the finding on [Signals](/enterprise/dashboard/signals) and preview again.

### RC-AI-03 — High and critical anomalies triaged within SLA

High and critical findings that are old enough to have missed the SLA (default 2 business days), looking back 30 days from that cut.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | At least 95% dispositioned on time |
| **warn** | At least 85% and below 95% |
| **fail** | Below 85% |
| **na** | No high or critical findings in the window |

**View population** lists those findings and whether they are still new or already dispositioned.

**Check it:** leave a high or critical finding open past the SLA, preview, and confirm **fail** or **warn**. Disposition it on Signals within the window and preview again for **pass**. With no such findings, the status stays **na**.

### RC-AI-04 — Hook tampering detected and resolved

AD-010 hook-tamper findings from the last 30 days. Pass requires every finding to be resolved within 5 days.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | 100% resolved within 5 days |
| **warn** | At least 80% and below 100% |
| **fail** | Below 80% |
| **na** | No hook-tamper findings |

**View population** lists the AD-010 findings.

**Check it:** on [Coverage](/enterprise/coverage), confirm there is no open tamper finding (**na** or **pass**). If one exists, resolve it within 5 days and preview. Leaving it open past 5 days fails the control.

### RC-AI-05 — LLM traffic through the approved gateway

Compares provider-reported tokens for the day with tokens on proxy sessions ingested that same UTC day. The control stays **na** until the org is Enterprise, has at least one gateway key that is not revoked, and provider usage reconciliation is on.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Unreconciled ratio ≤ 1% |
| **warn** | Above 1% and at or below 3% |
| **fail** | Above 3%, or both provider and gateway token totals are 0 |
| **na** | Not Enterprise, no active gateway key, or reconciliation is off |

Zero traffic after reconciliation is enabled is a **fail** (`no_proxy_traffic`). **View population** lists proxy sessions. With no traffic, the only row is the org id and the date.

**Check it:**

1. In org settings, turn on **Enable provider usage reconciliation** and save an Anthropic or OpenAI admin key.
2. Click **Compute preview now**. With reconciliation on and no usage or proxy tokens today, RC-AI-05 changes from **na** to **fail**.
3. After gateway traffic and a `provider_usage_daily` total land within 1% of each other, preview again for **pass**. A gap around 2% warns. A gap above 3% fails.

### RC-AI-07 — Quarterly access review completed

An access-review campaign that was completed and attested within 92 days.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Latest in-window campaign is attested and has no segregation-of-duties exceptions |
| **warn** | That campaign has at least one SoD self-review |
| **fail** | No completed and attested campaign in the window |

The fail row explains why (no campaign, still in progress, completed without attestation, or older than the window) and links to Access reviews. **View population** lists the review items from the latest attested campaign.

**Check it:** under **Access reviews**, create a campaign, decide every item, and complete it with attestation checked. Preview. RC-AI-07 should **pass**. Completing it without attestation, or letting the latest attested campaign age past 92 days, **fails**. A single-admin self-review records an SoD exception and **warns**.

### RC-AI-08 — Departed members lose access within 24 hours

Gateway and org API keys created by a removed member must be revoked within 24 hours of `member.removed`. Shared-key exposure items that stay open longer than 7 days warn even when nobody was removed that day.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Removals in the last 2 days, and every owned key revoked within 24 hours |
| **warn** | A shared-key exposure item is still open after 7 days |
| **fail** | An owned key is still active, or was revoked after 24 hours |
| **na** | No recent removals and no stale shared-key items |

**View population** lists the key ids (`ok` or `late`) or the stale shared-key items.

**Check it:** remove a test member who created a gateway or org API key. Revoke that key immediately and preview for **pass**. Leave the key active and preview for **fail**. After a real removal, decide the off-cycle shared-key items (rotate or keep) within 7 days so the control does not **warn**.

### RC-AI-09 — Security policy changes are logged and authorized

Policy and security audit events for the day must be performed by an admin. When a [SIEM destination](/enterprise/dashboard/siem) is enabled, delivery success for that day is included.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Every event has an admin actor, and SIEM delivery is at least 99% when SIEM is on |
| **warn** | Admin share is below 100% but within 5 points, or SIEM delivery is short while admins authored the events |
| **fail** | Admin share is more than 5 points below 100% |
| **na** | No policy or security audit events that day |

**View population** lists the audit event ids and whether the actor was an admin.

**Check it:** change a security or policy setting as an admin, then preview. The new event should be in the population with status **admin**, and the control should **pass** when SIEM is off or delivering. A day with no such changes stays **na**.

### RC-AI-10 — Retention is executed and legal hold is honored

Compliance retention stays at or above the Type II window default (400 days). Legal hold blocks purge.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | Compliance retention is at least 400 days and has not been lowered |
| **warn** | Retention was lowered, or the configured days are below 400 |

**View population** shows one row, **Retention policy**, with the reason. A lowered value also shows a banner on Control health.

**Check it:** leave **Compliance → Settings** at the default and preview for **pass**. Lower compliance retention, confirm the warning, and preview for **warn**. Turning legal hold on keeps purge blocked. The control stays **pass** while retention itself is still at the floor.

### RC-AI-11 — Security alerts are delivered

Share of alert deliveries created that day that reached `sent` or `skipped`.

| Result | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **pass** | At least 99% |
| **warn** | At least 94% and below 99% |
| **fail** | Below 94% |
| **na** | No alert deliveries that day |

**View population** lists each delivery id, tier, and status.

**Check it:** send a test alert from [Signals](/enterprise/dashboard/signals) or wait for a real finding email, then preview. A successful send **passes**. A day with no deliveries stays **na**.

## Access reviews

Admins create a campaign with a label, due date, and scope (gateway keys, org API keys, members). Items freeze a subject snapshot at creation. Team-scoped keys go to a team lead when possible; reviewers cannot decide their own access when another admin exists (single-admin self-review is allowed and recorded as an SoD exception → RC-AI-07 **warn**).

Decisions are `keep` / `revoke` / `modify` / `rotate` with a required reason. Completing a campaign requires attestation; the product stores `completed_by`, timestamp, and a SHA-256 of the decision set (no cryptographic signature).

Removing a member also creates off-cycle **shared key exposure** items for team gateway keys the departed member could use but did not create. Reviewers choose `rotate` or `keep` (with justification).

## Risk exceptions

A single register covers kinds such as `accepted_finding`, `dlp_exception`, and `control_threshold_override`. Every exception needs a rationale and an **expires_at** within 12 months of approval. On expiry, status becomes `expired` and reversible prior state is restored where applicable.

On [Signals](/enterprise/dashboard/signals), **Accept risk** on an anomaly finding creates an `accepted_finding` exception and dismisses the finding.
