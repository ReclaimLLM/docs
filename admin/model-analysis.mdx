Is your current model the right one for the job? Model Analysis replays your team's real requests against cheaper or better models, so you can compare them with evidence from your own workload before you switch. Results are scored by **category agreement** for classification answers or by a **blind LLM judge** for open-ended responses.

## Run a comparison

<Steps>
  <Step title="Freeze a dataset">
    Pick real sessions captured by your proxy and freeze them. Freezing makes no provider calls, so you get a stable, repeatable test set. Each dataset uses one scoring method: category agreement or a blind LLM judge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare a full run">
    Open **Prepare full run** on any frozen dataset. The panel shows the source baseline and, for judge datasets, the frozen judge model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose target models">
    Search the model catalog and tick the targets you want to test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preflight">
    The compatibility preflight makes no provider calls. It shows how many candidate and judge calls the run needs and the maximum attempts before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, filter, retry">
    Follow progress and results, filter by state or target model, and retry any failed cases. Open a case for its evidence panel and switch between **Changes**, **Side by side**, and **Raw diff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Before each replay the request is normalized: the model is replaced, streaming is turned off, unsupported fields are dropped, and credentials are removed. The replay then goes through your governed enterprise proxy, so every comparison follows your existing controls.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does freezing a dataset call any model provider?">
    No. Freezing only snapshots sessions your proxy already captured. Provider calls happen only after you confirm a run, and the preflight shows the call count first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which scoring method should I use?">
    Use category agreement when the original answer is a label or classification. Use the blind LLM judge for open-ended responses like summaries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are failed cases hidden from the score?">
    No. Cases that can't be scored are listed under Compatibility and failure reasons and stay in the denominator. You can retry them from the results view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
