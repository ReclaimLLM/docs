ReclaimLLM enterprise uses three roles to control what each person can see and do within your organization. Admins manage the org; team leads see their teams' activity; developers see their own sessions. Admins additionally control org-wide governance — session sharing, retention, and encryption — from the Settings tab.

## The three roles

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved">
    Full visibility across the org. Manages members, teams, settings, gateway credentials, encryption policy, and retention rules. Can see and (if permitted) open all sessions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Team Lead" icon="users-gear">
    Sees sessions and analytics for developers assigned to their team. Cannot manage org-level settings or view other teams' activity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Developer" icon="code">
    Sees their own captured sessions and any sessions explicitly shared with them. No access to the enterprise portal or other developers' activity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Adding members and assigning teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a developer">
    In the enterprise portal, open **Members** and click **Invite member**. Enter the developer's email address and select their role. They receive an email invitation to join the organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create teams">
    Go to **Teams** and click **New team**. Give the team a name that matches how your organization is structured — for example, `backend`, `data`, or `platform`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a team lead">
    Open the team and click **Assign team lead**. The team lead gains visibility into all sessions attributed to developers on that team from the moment of assignment forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add developers to the team">
    From the team page, add developers by selecting them from your org member list. Once assigned, sessions they capture are attributed to that team in org analytics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Backfill historical sessions (optional)">
    After assigning a developer to a team, you can trigger a backfill to attribute their previously captured sessions to your org. This ensures analytics reflect their full history, not just sessions captured after joining.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A developer can belong to one team at a time. Reassigning them to a new team updates attribution for future sessions; historical session attribution is not retroactively changed.
</Note>

## Org-wide governance settings

Beyond roles and team assignment, admins configure org-wide governance from the **Settings** tab: data retention and legal hold, session sharing scope (org-wide vs. team-only), org-wide session encryption and admin decrypt access, and project name normalization. See the [Settings tab reference](/enterprise/dashboard/settings) for the full breakdown of each control.

## SSO (SAML/OIDC)

SSO configuration is available in the enterprise portal under **Settings → SSO**. The following reflects the current state:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What's available now">
    - Store SAML or OIDC provider configuration for your organization.
    - Test the SSO connection from the portal.
    - Allow members to sign in via your identity provider.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Coming soon">
    - **Enforced SSO** — Require all org members to authenticate through your identity provider. Password login will be disabled for managed accounts.
    - **SCIM provisioning** — Automatically provision and deprovision org members when their status changes in your identity provider.
    - **Role sync** — Map identity provider groups to RCLM roles so role assignment stays in sync with your IdP.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  If SSO enforcement is critical for your deployment, contact [enterprise@rclm.ai](mailto:enterprise@rclm.ai). We can prioritize configuration support for your identity provider while the enforcement feature is in development.
</Info>

## Admin controls in the enterprise portal

The enterprise portal collects all org-level admin controls in one place. As an admin, you can access the following from the portal:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Member management">
    Invite new members, change existing member roles, and remove members from the org. Role changes take effect immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Team management">
    Create, rename, and delete teams. Assign team leads and add or remove developers from any team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Session sharing">
    Control whether members can view and search sessions across the whole org, or only within their own team. Sharing is always read-only — members can never edit, delete, or share a session they don't own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Project name mappings">
    Rename or merge project names org-wide. Org-level mappings take precedence over each member's own personal project naming, everywhere a project name is shown.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Encryption settings">
    Enable or disable org-wide encrypted raw session storage. Configure whether admins and team leads can open encrypted session details for org members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Retention policy">
    Set configurable retention windows for session data. A background task enforces deletions across session records and blobs on the schedule you define.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="LLM Gateway">
    Store provider credentials, create and revoke team gateway keys, and configure provider and model policy per key. See [LLM Gateway](/enterprise/llm-gateway) for full setup instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SSO configuration">
    Store and test your SAML or OIDC provider configuration. Enforced SSO is in development.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
