The Settings tab (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/settings`) collects org-wide governance controls that apply across every member and team. For the admin workflow, including enterprise deletion, see [Enterprise settings](/admin/settings).

## Data retention policy

Set a retention period in days (blank = retain indefinitely), or place the org under **legal hold** to override retention entirely and block deletion.

## Session sharing

Two independent toggles control who can see whose sessions:

- **Share across organization** — any member can view and search any other member's sessions.
- **Share within teams** — members can view and search sessions from others on their own team(s) (has no effect while org-wide sharing is on).

Sharing is always read-only — a member can never edit, delete, or share a session they don't own via this setting.

## Session encryption

- **Org-wide encryption** — new sessions are marked for background encryption going forward (existing sessions keep their current state until a backfill runs).
- **Admin decrypt access** — an Allow/Block toggle controlling whether org admins and team leads can open encrypted session details for other members. Owners and system jobs can always access their own data.

## Project name mappings

Rename or merge project names org-wide. These org-level names take precedence over each member's own personal project mappings, everywhere a project name is shown in the dashboard.

## SSO (SAML/OIDC)

A sub-page under Settings for configuring single sign-on: choose OIDC or SAML, paste provider config as JSON, and enable SSO for the org. See [Roles & Access → SSO](/enterprise/roles-access#sso-saml-oidc) for what's available now versus coming soon.

## Signals & alert policy

- **Session idle gap threshold** — sets the inactivity gap (default: 20 minutes) used by pattern P4 before a wait event is counted.
- **Enforce supported compression** — switches installed hooks from observe-only to compression enforcement on their next session start.
- **Email alerts & cadence** — configures org routing (security inbox, recipient inboxes, organization timezone), delivery cadences (immediate P0, daily/weekly digests at 08:00 recipient-local time), minimum severity, included detectors and signals, project exclusions, and the **AD-015** allowed egress safe list (trusted URLs, domains, IPs, and CIDR ranges). Personal preferences live under **My alerts** on the Signals tab. See [Signals Dashboard → Email alerts & notification cadence](/enterprise/dashboard/signals#email-alerts--notification-cadence).

## Coverage & device policy

Hook integrity and DLP posture are managed primarily from the [Coverage tab](/enterprise/dashboard/coverage). From Settings (or Coverage settings) admins can:

- **Require DLP** — overrides local `--no-dlp` unless an unexpired exception applies
- **Require heartbeat** — installs the `rclm-heartbeat` OS timer on developer machines
- Configure gateway hosts / required providers used by AD-013 routing checks
- (Enterprise) enable provider usage reconciliation for AD-013c

See [Coverage Alerts](/enterprise/coverage) for enrollment, privacy, and detector overview.

## Compliance retention

Compliance metadata retention (snapshots, campaigns, exceptions) is managed under **Compliance → Settings**. Lowering the floor requires confirmation, shows a banner on Control health, and makes RC-AI-10 warn. See [Compliance dashboard](/enterprise/dashboard/compliance#compliance-settings).

## Delete the enterprise

The bottom of Settings schedules a 30-day deletion for every current member. Sessions are hidden and API keys are revoked immediately. An admin can cancel during the grace period. Legal hold blocks the action. See [Delete the enterprise](/admin/settings#delete-the-enterprise).

## Who sees it

Admin only, including the SSO sub-page.
