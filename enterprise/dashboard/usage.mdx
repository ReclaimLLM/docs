The Usage tab (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/usage`) answers "how much AI activity is happening, and what kind."

## What you'll see

- A bar chart of **Sessions**, **Input Tokens**, or **Output Tokens** over time, toggle between **day** and **hour** granularity, with peak-value and hover tooltips.
- **Sessions by Category** — a Fix / Feature / Performance breakdown of CLI sessions.
- **Usage by Model Group** table — Group, Sessions, Tokens (in/out), Files Modified, Lines (\+/-), Files Read.
- **Detailed Usage** table (paginated, 10 rows/page) — Time, Model, Sessions, Files, Lines (\+/-), Touches.
- **Sessions by Project** — top 10 projects by session count.

## What you can do

Filter by date range (default: last 30 days, with a one-click "Last 30 days" reset) and granularity; page through the detailed table.

## Who sees it

Visible to any role with portal access.

For how this compares to [Tokens](/enterprise/dashboard/tokens) and [Stats](/enterprise/dashboard/stats), see [Observability](/enterprise/observability).
