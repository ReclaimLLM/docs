The Teams tab (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/teams`) is where admins and team leads manage teams and who belongs to the org. The old Members URL (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/members`) opens this page.

## What you'll see

- **Create Team** — admins only.
- **Send invite** — team leads only. Sends a developer invite.
- One card per team, with a members table (Name, Email, Role, Joined, Last session) and pending invites for that team.
- **Not on a team** — org members who are not on any team. Admins can change their role or remove them from the org here.
- **Pending invites** — invites that are not attached to a team.

## What you can do

| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Team** | Admin |
| **+ Add member** on a team | Admin. An existing org member is added to that team. A new email gets an org invite for that team, as a developer. |
| **Change role** | Admin, from the Role dropdown on the member's row |
| **Remove from team** | Admin |
| **Remove from the org** | Admin, from **Not on a team** |
| **Send invite** | Team lead. Invites a developer without picking a team. |
| **Revoke** or **Cancel** | Drops a pending invite |
| **View Signals** | Opens that team's Signals view |

<Note>
  Gateway keys on the [API Keys](/enterprise/dashboard/api-keys) tab must be assigned to a team, so teams need to exist before an admin can scope a gateway key.
</Note>
