The Teams tab (`/enterprise/orgs/{org_id}/teams`) is a per-team scoped view over the same membership data as [Members](/enterprise/dashboard/members).

## What you'll see

- A **Create Team** input at the top.
- One card per team, each with its own members table (Name, Email, Role, Joined, Last session) and any pending team invites.

## What you can do

Create a team; add a member to a team by email (existing org members are added directly, new emails trigger a combined org \+ team invite); remove a member from a team; revoke a pending team invite; change a team member's role.

## Who sees it

Admin only.

<Note>
  Gateway keys on the [API Keys](/enterprise/dashboard/api-keys) tab must be assigned to a team, so teams need to exist before an admin can scope a gateway key.
</Note>
