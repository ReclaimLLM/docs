Personal plans are managed from **Settings**. Switching Pro to Free stops the next payment. Deleting the account is a separate 30-day process that removes the account and its personal data.

Enterprise members cannot cancel their own plan or delete their own account. An organization admin does that from [Enterprise settings](/admin/settings#delete-the-enterprise).

## Switch Pro to Free

Open [Settings](https://reclaimllm.com/settings) and select **Free** on the plan control. Pro stays active until 24 hours before the next payment collection, and that payment is not collected. Settings shows the date as **Moves to Free**.

<Warning>
  If the next payment collection is already within 24 hours, Pro ends immediately so that payment is not collected. There is no proration charge and no final invoice.
</Warning>

ReclaimLLM emails you when the switch is scheduled, when it happens immediately, and when you keep Pro.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to [reclaimllm.com/settings](https://reclaimllm.com/settings). The plan control is at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Free">
    Confirm the date in the dialog. **Billing details** on the same page shows the next payment collection date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Pro if you change your mind">
    While the switch is scheduled, select **Keep Pro**. The next payment is collected on the original date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Paid tier stays in place until the scheduled time. After that, the account is Free. Captured sessions remain on the account.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is at the bottom of Settings. It is available on Free and Pro accounts that are not members of an active enterprise.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Delete account">
    Select **Delete account...** and type `DELETE`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Confirm Schedule Deletion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the account during the grace period">
    You can still sign in. A banner on the dashboard and Settings shows that deletion is pending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Scheduling deletion does this immediately:

- Personal sessions are hidden
- Personal API keys are revoked
- A paid subscription stops renewing
- ReclaimLLM emails you the permanent-deletion date

You have 30 days to select **Cancel Deletion & Keep Account** in Settings, or **Cancel Deletion & Restore** on the banner. Sessions that were hidden come back. Revoked API keys are not recreated; create new keys if you still need them.

After 30 days the account, sessions, and stored files are permanently removed. That cannot be undone.

<Note>
  Organization sessions are not deleted by a personal account deletion. Enterprise members must ask an admin to [delete the enterprise](/admin/settings#delete-the-enterprise).
</Note>
